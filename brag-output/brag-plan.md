# TejaDev Template — launch film plan

**What it is:** a production-ready full-stack TypeScript monorepo (Next.js 16 + Express on Bun, Turborepo) with auth, database, UI, logging and tooling already wired.
**Who it's for:** developers who lose the first days of every new project to the same setup.
**Hook:** "Day 1 · every new project": the chores everyone repeats appear one by one on the beat, then get struck through.
**Tone:** cinematic and confident. Cream + deep-green brand (from `apps/web/src/app/globals.css`), Hanken Grotesk display type, Instrument Serif accents, JetBrains Mono for the terminal.
**Music:** "Happy Beats / Business Moves" vol. 10 by ende.app (110 BPM, bundled with the /brag skill). Every cut sits on its beat grid, and the end card lands on the strong cue at 18.01 s. UI foley (Kenney) is tucked under the music.
